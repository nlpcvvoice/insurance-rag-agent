# Commercial Auto Insurance Overview

## What Is Commercial Auto Insurance?

Commercial auto insurance covers vehicles used for business purposes, including delivery vans, work trucks, company cars, and vehicles with employees behind the wheel. Personal auto policies typically exclude business use, so businesses need a commercial policy.

## Important Coverage Components

### Business Auto Liability
Covers bodily injury and property damage to others caused by vehicles you own or operate for business. It generally covers medical bills, lost wages, and property repair for third parties.

### Physical Damage Coverage
- **Comprehensive**: covers loss from theft, fire, vandalism, flood, and other non-collision events
- **Collision**: covers damage to your vehicle from hitting another object or vehicle
- Coverage is often subject to a deductible and market value limits

### Medical Payments and PIP
Pays medical expenses for the driver and passengers in the business vehicle regardless of fault. In no-fault states, personal injury protection (PIP) also covers lost wages and related costs.

### Uninsured and Underinsured Motorist Coverage
Protects you if a business vehicle is hit by a driver without insurance or with insufficient limits to cover the damages.

## Who Needs It

Commercial auto policies are needed by delivery and courier services, contractors, plumbers and electricians with work trucks, rental fleets, and any business with vehicles titled to the company or used regularly for commerce.

## Fleet Policies

Businesses with several vehicles often choose a fleet policy, which schedules multiple vehicles under one agreement and can streamline claims and premium management across the entire fleet.
